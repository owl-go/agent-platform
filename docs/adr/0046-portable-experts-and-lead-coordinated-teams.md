# Portable Experts and lead-coordinated Expert Teams

On 2026-10-09, the User confirmed three architectural directions: Experts and Expert Teams support portable packages using a neutral `.plugin/plugin.json` entry; each Expert has one authoritative Markdown guidance document and a separate display-only Introduction; Expert Teams use a Team Lead that delegates according to the task and produces the official response, with platform-enforced delegation, concurrency, and consumption limits. This makes specialist definitions portable and allows teams to use the members needed for a task rather than invoking every member unconditionally. A creation wizard may generate the same guidance document but must not maintain a second authoritative instruction representation.

The User also confirmed team-owned member definitions copied from catalog Experts, package-scoped bundled Skills, separately resolved and authorized external Connector dependencies, lossless conversion of legacy Expert fields, explicit Team Lead selection when upgrading old teams, and preservation of historical snapshot execution semantics. Owned definitions prevent source-profile edits, deletion, and permission changes from silently rewriting a team. Package-scoped Skills avoid adding every imported dependency to the global catalog.

Parallel members use independent Workspace copies, with checked merges and conflicting changes returned to the Team Lead. The defaults are three concurrent members, twenty model invocations including Team Lead calls, and a two-hour overall execution timeout. An optional per-response Credit budget is checked before each invocation; actual usage may exceed a reservation in the final admitted invocation, after which further calls stop. A failed delegated task permits at most one new delegation under the same overall limits. Failure of required work fails the entire response and prevents Workspace promotion; a User retry restarts from frozen inputs, while external side effects must be surfaced because Workspace rollback cannot undo them.

Only Administrators may create Expert Teams. This applies to manual, conversational, import, and copy paths; a UI restriction alone is insufficient. Ordinary Users may select available platform teams with their own execution settings and authorizations. The User explicitly chose deletion of existing ordinary-User-owned private team definitions, rather than read-only retention or continued maintenance. Migration deletes those definitions and clears mutable references without publishing or inspecting them; historical conversations and immutable execution snapshots retain their original private content and execution semantics.

The confirmed package lifecycle uses neutral ZIP import/export, an explicit schema version, a stable package identity, semantic versioning, and immutable content revisions. Identical imports are idempotent; conflicting content under the same identity and version is rejected. Editing and conversational creation operate on the same authoritative guidance, optional profile images, and up to three starter prompts. The User subsequently removed automatic tag generation and separate classification: capability and applicability remain authored content in Introduction and Expert Guidance.

The Team Lead returns validated structured delegation or completion actions. The platform executes members from the frozen roster, supplies task-scoped context, and returns member results and file-change summaries to the lead. Members cannot form nested teams. Execution does not depend on native subagents or a shared internal MCP implementation. Runtime and exact-image acceptance still requires independent evidence.

Limit exhaustion fails explicitly and stops remaining work. The two-hour budget measures active execution; it pauses only when all execution waits for User approval, whose individual expiry remains at most fifteen minutes. Worker interruption fails the interrupted response, retains usage and safe operation facts, and leaves retry to the User. The Task Panel projects real delegation, member state, invocation count, consumption, file conflicts, and expandable member results; the Team Lead alone provides the official answer.

The confirmed design remains pending final shared-understanding review in [the refactor design](../product/expert-package-team-refactor.md). This proposal will replace ADR-0022's fixed-order execution and final-member response decision, ADR-0026's independently authored structured guidance fields, and the product's private-team creation and automatic discovery-tag rules after that review. The existing implementation still follows the earlier contracts; this record claims no implementation, deployment, or conformance evidence.
